NOTICE: Citizens Earning Below $100,000 Are Required to Enroll in the Mandatory Vasectomy Program.

About the Initiative

As part of our commitment to a sustainable future, all eligible male citizens over the age of 18 with annual incomes below $100,000 are required to undergo a vesectomy. This is in accordance with new federal legislation to ensure responsible economic stewardship of reproductive resources. This helps protect our nation's health, security, and economic viability for future generations.

This program is designed to ensure a balanced future for all Americans. Thank you for your commitment to civic duty and national responsibility.
